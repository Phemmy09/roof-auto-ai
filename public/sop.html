Purpose & Scope

This Standard Operating Procedure defines how Reliable Exteriors Group staff should use the Roof Auto AI system to generate material orders, crew instructions, and labor item documentation for roofing jobs.

This SOP applies to:

  • Project Managers (PMs) — responsible for initiating jobs and reviewing output
  • Office Staff — responsible for uploading documents and distributing the PDF
  • Operations Managers — responsible for adjusting formulas and quality review
ℹ️
This system is for internal use only. Roof Auto AI is a private application — do not share the URL with customers or external parties.

Upload Documents

Click the upload area or drag files onto it to add documents. You can add multiple files at once.

  • Signed contract PDF

Document Preparation Guide

Eagle View Report

This is the most critical document. The AI extracts these fields from it:

  • Squares (total roof area in SQ)
  • Pitch (slope — e.g. 4/12, 6/12)
  • Ridge, Hip, Valley, Rake, Eave lengths (in linear feet)
  • Pipe boot count
⚠️
Do not rename the Eagle View PDF to anything containing the words photo, before, image, etc. — it will be filtered out and not sent to Claude AI, resulting in zero material quantities.

Contract PDF

The AI reads the contract to determine:

  • Contract type (Retail vs. Insurance)
  • Shingle product name and color
  • Any special items (skylights, solar panels, swamp coolers, chimney)
  • Ventilation strategy (ridge, box, or hybrid)

If the contract is a multi-page scan, include the full PDF. The AI reads all pages.

City / County Code Document

This tells the AI what local code requirements apply (permit requirements, mid-roof inspection thresholds, decking standards, ice & water requirements). Use the REG-prepared City/County Code Compliance Guide PDF for the relevant jurisdiction.

Compressing Large PDFs

If a PDF exceeds 4 MB, use one of these free tools before uploading:

  • Adobe Acrobat → File → Reduce File Size
  • Smallpdf.com → Compress PDF
  • ilovepdf.com → Compress PDF

Target file size: under 2 MB per document for fastest processing.

Crew Instructions

These are ordered, job-specific steps generated from the AI's reading of all documents. They reflect:

  • The specific jurisdiction's permit and inspection requirements
  • Ventilation strategy for this roof
  • Special items (solar panels, swamp coolers, chimneys, skylights)
  • Tear-off rules and decking inspection
  • Photography requirements for the job
  • Site protection and safety setup
🔴
Always review crew instructions before distributing to the crew. AI-generated content is accurate for standard cases, but a PM should review for any unusual site conditions not captured in the uploaded documents.

Labor Items

These are the billable line items for the job — tear-off per layer, story charges, steep slope, skylight, permit fee, etc. They should be cross-referenced with the Xactimate scope or retail contract before finalizing the invoice.

Material Notes

Notes appear in blue below the Material Order Form on screen and in the PDF. They include:

  • Shingle product name, color, and color reference links
  • Drip edge, pipe boot, and flashing color specs
  • Supplement flags — items that may require a supplemental charge (e.g. code-required drip edge not in scope, mid-roof inspection fee)
  • Confirmation notes (e.g. "No valleys — no valley metal needed")

Navigate to /formula-engine in the address bar.

Quality Control Checklist

After every processed job, a PM or Office Manager should verify the following before distributing results to the crew or placing orders.

Extracted Data Check

  • Customer name and address match the contract
  • Squares match the Eagle View total area (±1 SQ is acceptable)
  • Pitch matches the Eagle View dominant pitch
  • Contract type (Retail / Insurance) is correct
  • Shingle product and color match the signed contract
  • Pipe boot count matches the Eagle View or site survey
  • Special items (solar panels, skylights, swamp cooler, chimney) correctly identified

Material Quantities Check

  • Field shingles ≈ Eagle View squares × 1.10 (rounded up)
  • No material quantity shows as 0 unexpectedly (indicates extraction failure)
  • Drip edge totals make sense given the roof perimeter
  • Sealant shows at least 3 tubes
  • Starter strip shows at least 3 bundles

Crew Instructions Check

  • Permit requirement is mentioned (if applicable to the jurisdiction)
  • Mid-roof inspection is flagged for low-slope roofs (under 4:12 pitch)
  • Ventilation strategy matches the contract (Ridge, Box, or Hybrid)
  • Any solar panels / skylights / swamp coolers have specific handling instructions
  • Photo requirements are included for CompanyCam
  • No obvious errors or mismatched addresses appear in the instructions

Supplement Flags Check

  • Review all items marked "SUPPLEMENT FLAG" in Material Notes
  • Confirm any flagged items are added to the invoice or supplement where applicable
🔴
If any material shows 0 SQ or 0 LF unexpectedly, the Eagle View was likely not processed. Check that the Eagle View file is not named with words like "photo" and re-run the job.
10

Troubleshooting

🔴 All material quantities show 0

This usually means the Eagle View was not read by the AI.

  1. Check the Eagle View filename — does it contain any of these words? photo, before, progress, completion, after, image, pic, drone
  2. If yes, rename the file and re-run the job
  3. If no, check if the file is over 4 MB — compress it and re-upload
  4. If still failing, note the error message and contact IT
🔴 "Server error" or blank screen during processing
  1. Wait 60 seconds and refresh the page
  2. Try re-running the job with fewer or smaller files
  3. If the error message says "Claude AI error", the AI service may be temporarily down — wait 5 minutes and try again
  4. If the problem persists, contact IT with the exact error message shown
🟡 Processing takes more than 3 minutes
  1. Do not close the browser tab — the job is still running
  2. Large PDFs (2–4 MB) are the most common cause of slow processing
  3. If it exceeds 5 minutes with no result, the job has likely timed out — refresh and try again with compressed files
🟡 Email not received by Cheryl
  1. Check the results screen — does it say "email sent"?
  2. If yes, ask Cheryl to check her spam/junk folder. The sender is izzy.marketing.hub@gmail.com
  3. If the screen says email failed, download the PDF from the results screen and email it to cheryl@therelexgroup.com manually
  4. Notify IT if emails continue to fail
🟡 "File too large" error or file won't upload
  1. The file exceeds 10 MB (browser limit) — compress it using Smallpdf.com or Adobe Acrobat
  2. Target under 2 MB per file for best performance
  3. Photos from a phone may be very large — use PDF compression before uploading photo PDF compilations
🟡 Shingle color or product is wrong in the output
  1. Verify the signed contract clearly states the product and color — scan quality can affect AI reading accuracy
  2. If the contract has a low-resolution scan, run it through an online PDF enhancer or re-scan at 300 DPI
  3. You can note the correct product manually when forwarding to the crew — the AI output is a starting point, not a replacement for PM review
🔴 "Next Step" button is grayed out on Step 1

The email field contains an invalid address. Ensure:

  • The email field contains cheryl@therelexgroup.com (or another valid email)
  • There are no extra spaces before or after the address
  • The Customer Name field is filled in
🟡 Formula Engine shows "Error updating formulas"
  1. This means the database table may not exist yet. Contact IT to run the Supabase schema setup script.
  2. Your changes were NOT saved — do not proceed with jobs until this is resolved

Glossary

TermDefinition
SQ (Square)Unit of roofing measurement. 1 SQ = 100 square feet of roof area.
LF (Linear Foot)Length measurement — used for ridges, hips, valleys, rakes, eaves, and flashing.
Eagle ViewSatellite-based roof measurement report generated by EagleView Technologies. The primary source for all roof dimensions.
SquaresTotal roof area measured by Eagle View. Used as the base for all material calculations.
PitchRoof slope expressed as rise-over-run (e.g. 6/12 = 6 inches of rise per 12 inches of horizontal run).
RidgeThe horizontal peak of a roof where two slopes meet at the top.
HipA sloped external corner where two roof sections meet diagonally.
ValleyThe internal angle where two roof sections meet — water drains through valleys.
RakeThe sloped edge of a roof at the gable end.
EaveThe lower horizontal edge of a roof, typically above the gutters.
I&W / Ice & Water ShieldSelf-adhering waterproofing membrane installed at eaves, valleys, and penetrations.
Drip EdgeMetal strip installed at roof edges to direct water away from fascia.
Pipe Boot / Pipe JackFlashing boots installed around pipe penetrations (plumbing vents).
Starter StripFirst course of shingles (or dedicated starter product) installed at eaves and rakes.
SupplementAn additional charge submitted to an insurance company for items not included in the original scope.
Mid-Roof InspectionA city inspection required at certain stages of installation (before shingles are laid) in specific jurisdictions.
Formula EngineThe admin page at /formula-engine where material coverage constants are configured.
Claude AIThe artificial intelligence model (Anthropic Claude Sonnet 4.6) used to read and extract data from job documents.
